FAQ

The questions procurement always asks.

What counts as one "chat"?
A conversation, not a message. One customer talking to you within a rolling 24-hour window counts once, no matter how many messages fly back and forth or how many agents touch it. If the same person writes again three days later, that is a second conversation.
What happens if we go over our volume?
Nothing breaks. Extra conversations bill at your plan's overage rate (Rp 1.800 / 1.500 / 1.300) on the next invoice. If you cross the next tier's volume two months in a row, we move you up automatically — because the tier is always cheaper than the overage.
Are Meta's WhatsApp fees included?
No, and be suspicious of anyone who says they are. Meta bills per 24-hour conversation session by category (marketing, utility, service). We pass that through at cost with zero markup and show it as a separate line on your invoice, so you can audit it against Meta's own billing.
Can we add more phone numbers later?
Yes, at any time. Starter includes one WhatsApp Business number, Growth three, Scale five. Extra numbers are Rp 450.000 / 400.000 / 350.000 per number per month depending on plan, prorated to your billing date. Custom plans get unlimited numbers.
Do we need a new phone number, or can we keep ours?
Keep yours. We migrate an existing number onto the WhatsApp Business API in about two working days, including the one-time deregistration from the WhatsApp Business app. Chat history on the handset stays on the handset — that part Meta does not let anyone move.
How long does onboarding take?
Most teams are live the next working day: connect channels, import contacts, set routing rules. Autopilot needs your catalogue and price list and reaches usable accuracy within about a week of supervised use, where it drafts and your agents approve.
Is Autopilot going to say something embarrassing to a customer?
It runs in suggest-only mode until you say otherwise, and even then it only auto-answers the intents you switch on. It cannot promise discounts, invent stock or quote a price that is not in your catalogue, and any reply below your confidence threshold goes to a human with the draft attached.
Can we pay in rupiah with a local invoice?
Yes — bank transfer, virtual account, or card. You get a proper faktur pajak, and annual contracts can be split into quarterly billing on request. Government and BUMN procurement terms are supported on Custom.
What if we outgrow Scale?
That is Custom, and it starts around Rp 950 per conversation with volume steps below that. It also unlocks private cloud or on-premise deployment, a fine-tuned Autopilot model, and an SLA with financial penalties.
